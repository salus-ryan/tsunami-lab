About Tsunami Lab

This sandbox advances mass and momentum on a staggered, depth-varying shallow-water grid. A progressive multi-patch finite fault uses earthquake moment, rupture area, heterogeneous slip, rake, strike, dip, and focal-depth coupling. Deterministic source ensembles expose sensitivity to uncertain fault parameters.

What is modeled

  • One-degree global terrain-derived ocean depths adjusted for tidal stage
  • Mass-conserving face transports and adaptive CFL time steps
  • Progressive rupture at an approximate 2.6 km/s
  • Depth-dependent wave speed, bottom friction, Coriolis rotation, and source uncertainty ranges

Important limitations

  • One-degree cells cannot resolve bays, harbors, or local inundation
  • The finite fault is empirical—not an Okada elastic-dislocation solution
  • Coastal values use a simple shoaling proxy, not building-level damage
  • Regional tide forecasts, dispersion, nonlinear run-up, and wetting/drying are omitted

Terrain source: Mapzen Terrarium global elevation tiles (NASA SRTM and ocean bathymetry sources). Coastline: Natural Earth. Neither dataset implies endorsement.
