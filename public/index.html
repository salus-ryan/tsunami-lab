About Tsunami Lab

This sandbox solves a linear, depth-varying shallow-water wave equation on a coarse global grid. Earthquake moment, empirical rupture area, slip, dip, mechanism, and focal-depth coupling create an idealized initial sea-surface displacement.

What is real

  • Global terrain-derived ocean depths
  • Wave speed varies approximately as √(gravity × depth)
  • Continents reflect and channel waves
  • Earthquake scaling follows standard moment relations

Important limitations

  • Two-degree cells cannot model bays, harbors, or local inundation
  • The source is idealized—not a finite-fault reconstruction
  • Coastal values use a simple shoaling proxy, not building-level damage
  • Tides, dispersion, friction, and nonlinear run-up are omitted

Terrain source: Mapzen Terrarium global elevation tiles (NASA SRTM and ocean bathymetry sources). Coastline: Natural Earth. Neither dataset implies endorsement.
