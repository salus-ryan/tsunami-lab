Tsunami Lab

Privacy
PRIVACY

Your simulations stay yours

Tsunami Lab is a client-side educational simulator. It does not require an account and does not operate an application server or advertising system.

Data collection

The application does not collect names, email addresses, precise device locations, simulation settings, or exported results. It includes no analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking cookies.

Data stored on your device

Saved scenarios are stored in your browser's local storage. The offline app shell, terrain, and coastline are stored by the browser's service-worker cache. You can remove both through your browser's site-data controls. Uninstalling the PWA may not automatically remove browser site data on every platform.

Shared links and exports

Share links encode scenario settings in the URL fragment after #scenario=. URL fragments are not sent to the web host during normal HTTP requests. If you share the link, recipients and any apps used to share it can read those settings. Result exports are generated locally and downloaded directly by your browser.

Hosting and external links

The public application is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard connection information, such as IP addresses and request metadata, under its own privacy terms. Links to support resources leave Tsunami Lab and are governed by the destination's policies.

Children and safety

No personal information is intentionally requested. The simulator is educational and must not be used for emergency decisions, evacuation planning, or operational forecasting.

Last updated: September 5, 2026. This notice should be reviewed before adding accounts, payments, analytics, cloud sync, or other network services.
